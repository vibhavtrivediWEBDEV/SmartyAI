# Audio Files Guide - Legal Requirements

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Audio Licensing Warning

**You CANNOT legally download and use sounds from MyInstants without proper licensing.**

### Why This Is Important:
1. **Copyright Infringement** - Most MyInstants sounds are copyrighted material
2. **Redistribution Rights** - You need explicit permission to use sounds in your app
3. **DMCA Risk** - Unauthorized use can lead to takedowns
4. **Legal Liability** - Potential lawsuits for copyright violation

## ⚖️ Legal Compliance Checklist

Before using any sound:

- [ ] **Source identified** - Where did it come from?
- [ ] **License verified** - What does the license allow?
- [ ] **Commercial use OK?** - Can use in commercial app?
- [ ] **Redistribution allowed?** - Can bundle with app?
- [ ] **Attribution required?** - Need to give credit?
- [ ] **Modifications allowed?** - Can edit the sound?

**If ANY answer is "no" or "unclear" → DO NOT USE**
